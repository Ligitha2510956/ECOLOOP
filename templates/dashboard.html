Why this exists

Built by students who kept buying what the lab next door already had.

Talking to 15 students and 2 faculty across SSN, the same story kept coming up: usable items thrown away, expired chemicals nobody knew existed, duplicate purchases every semester. So we built one platform for chemicals, e-waste, and everyday things like textbooks, furniture and bicycles.

It stays purely digital — no new hardware, no extra budget, and listing an item takes under three clicks.
