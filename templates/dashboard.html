About EcoLoop

A campus platform for reuse.

EcoLoop helps students and departments keep useful resources circulating instead of purchasing duplicates or discarding usable items.

How it works

Lend or donate → others discover the item → a borrower sends a request → the owner reviews it → the borrower tracks the request through approval, pickup and completion.
